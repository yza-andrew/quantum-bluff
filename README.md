# quantum-bluff
A final project demo of CCST 9077

A bluffing card game for 2–4 players. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser. The game runs offline in one file.

Choose one human against computer opponents or local pass-and-play. Set a target, play up to three cards face down, and decide whether to follow or challenge. Quantum cards have two possible faces, and the encoding and observation bases determine how they are revealed. Clear your hand and pass the final observation to win.

This playable prototype uses quantum-inspired game rules. Balance is under playtest.
