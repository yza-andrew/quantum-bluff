Game setup

Table rules

  1. Two to four players start with six cards each. The deck contains 36 classical cards, 16 quantum cards and six each of H and X gates.
  2. The leader chooses the target. Claim one to three data cards face down. Click a gate, then a data card to attach it, or drag it onto the card. Click an attached gate to detach. Each gate leaves your hand with its data card.
  3. Choose + or × to encode a quantum play. The next player follows or challenges. Only the latest play is measured. Same basis reveals the encoded face. Different bases independently choose either face with equal probability.
  4. H adds a second off-target face or restores the first face of a quantum card. X swaps ♥↔♠ and ♦↔♣ and maps rank to 14 − rank. Seven keeps its rank. These poker mappings are game rules.
  5. If any card misses, its owner takes the whole play. Otherwise the challenger takes it. Revealed cards become classical. The loser draws one if available and leads. Older plays are removed.
  6. Emptying your hand forces observation. All cards must match to win. A gate-only hand draws until it finds data. With an empty deck, gates may be played as always-false blanks. A caught blank returns as a random classical card so play can continue.
